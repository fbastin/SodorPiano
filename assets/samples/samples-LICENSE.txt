Salmander Grand Piano sample library
=====================================

The audio sample files in this directory (assets/samples/*.mp3) are taken
from the "Salamander Grand Piano" sample library by Alessandro Iafrati,
distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC-BY-3.0) license.

  https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

A subset of the samples is used here: 30 recorded notes (A0, C1, D#1, F#1,
A1, ... up to C8) x 3 velocity layers. The files were down-sampled/selected,
tail-trimmed and re-encoded as MP3 for browser compatibility; the underlying
recordings remain property of their author and are used under CC-BY-3.0.

Note: because '#' is a URL fragment separator, sharp note names are stored
with 's' (e.g. F#3 -> Fs3).

Attribution: Alessandro Iafrati, "Salamander Grand Piano".
